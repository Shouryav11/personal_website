Experience

My journey as a learner has been a mix of curiosity, challenges, and continuous growth across multiple domains. I started by exploring Web Development, where I gained hands-on experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C, and Flask. This phase taught me how the internet works behind the scenes and how creative and logical thinking come together to build something interactive. I faced challenges in debugging errors and aligning designs, but these obstacles strengthened my patience and problem-solving abilities.

Parallel to that, I dove into the world of Data Science, where I worked with Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Excel, and Power BI. I learned how to clean messy datasets, analyze them, and transform raw numbers into meaningful visual insights. Projects like sales analysis and YouTube dataset exploration made me realize the power of data-driven decision-making. At times, I struggled with complex visualizations and model accuracy, but gradually, I developed the skill of approaching problems step by step.

As a Mechatronics student, I also engaged in practical projects such as robot kinematics, mechanical design, and robotics basics. Mini projects like the oil skimmer RC boat and Fusion 360 CAD models gave me exposure to applying mechanical and electronic concepts together. These experiences showed me how theory transforms into real engineering solutions, even within a tight budget. Working in groups taught me collaboration and leadership, while balancing cost and creativity in projects.

My recent exposure to Generative AI opened a whole new dimension. From building a command-line chatbot to experimenting with AI video tools like Kling AI, I learned how AI can merge creativity with automation. It showed me that AI is not just a futuristic concept but a practical tool for solving real problems and creating content.

Freelancing also became a part of my journey, where I tried to merge all these skills — design, coding, and AI — into small projects. This gave me a taste of the professional side of things: deadlines, client expectations, and the importance of presenting work effectively.

Looking back, every step of this journey — whether it was fixing bugs in code, building a CAD model, debugging a machine learning model, or experimenting with AI content creation — added a new layer of growth. I learned resilience, adaptability, and the courage to explore new domains without fear. My path is not just about technical skills, but about developing a creative, problem-solving mindset that keeps pushing me forward.
